About

Aquariums, saltwater fish, and trusted local help since 2010.

Rob's Aquatics in Tinley Park has served local aquarium customers since 2010. The shop is known for aquariums, live saltwater fish, fish tanks, supplies and food, live corals, and the kind of practical advice that helps customers make smart decisions for their setup.

Whether you're starting a new aquarium, upgrading equipment, or refining an established saltwater system, the goal is simple: healthy fish, dependable equipment, and straightforward support.

We carry saltwater fish, live corals, filtration systems, pumps, heaters, frozen fish food, reef supplements, pre-mixed salt water, and other trusted products for successful aquarium care.

FAQ

Common questions from local aquarium customers

Straight answers to help you plan your visit, your setup, and your next upgrade.

Do you specialize in saltwater aquariums?

Yes. Rob's Aquatics specializes in saltwater fish, live corals, reef supplies, and support for saltwater aquarium systems.

Can you help a beginner?

Yes. We can recommend tank size, filtration, lighting, and marine livestock options that fit your goals, space, and budget—without the guesswork.

Do you offer help with full aquarium setup or installation?

Yes. Rob's Aquatics can help with setup planning and works closely with Sea Life Service for aquarium installation support.

Can I call ahead to check availability?

Yes. Call (708) 444-7627 to confirm current livestock or product availability before visiting.

Can you help troubleshoot algae or water quality problems?

Absolutely. Bring your tank details, equipment information, and water parameters if available. The team can help diagnose common algae, chemistry, and livestock compatibility issues.
