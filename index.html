About

Marine-focused service with practical, local experience.

Since 2010, Rob's Aquatics has served saltwater hobbyists only—quality marine livestock, dependable reef hardware, and straightforward recommendations. We do not stock or service fresh water aquariums, so every conversation stays focused on marine success. Whether you are planning your first saltwater setup or refining an established reef, the goal is a stable, thriving tank.

Our approach is practical and straightforward (no pressure, no gimmicks). If you're new to marine tanks, we'll walk you through what actually matters for a healthy system.

We carry a carefully selected range of saltwater fish, corals, marine equipment, filtration, lighting, supplements, and reef supplies—products we trust and stand behind.

FAQ

Common questions from local saltwater and reef hobbyists

Straight answers to help you plan your visit, your setup, and your next upgrade.

Do you carry fresh water fish or plants?

No. Rob's Aquatics is saltwater and reef only—we don't stock fresh water livestock, plants, or fresh water supplies. Everything in the store is geared toward marine aquariums.

Can you help with a beginner saltwater setup?

Yes. We can recommend tank size, filtration, lighting, and marine livestock options that fit your goals, space, and budget—without the guesswork.

Do you offer help with full aquarium setup or installation?

Rob's Aquatics can provide setup guidance and, when needed, coordinate with trusted local installation partners for more involved builds.

Can I call ahead to check availability?

Yes. Call (708) 444-7627 to confirm current livestock or product availability before visiting.

Can you help troubleshoot algae or water quality problems?

Absolutely. Bring your tank details, equipment information, and water parameters if available. The team can help diagnose common algae, chemistry, and livestock compatibility issues.
